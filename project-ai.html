Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Understanding Context

Implemented attention mechanisms and transformer-based models (BERT, GPT) to maintain conversation context over multiple turns, enabling more coherent and relevant responses.

Challenge: Handling Edge Cases

Built a confidence scoring system with human-in-the-loop training. Low-confidence conversations are escalated to human agents, providing feedback to improve the model.

Challenge: Multi-language Support

Implemented multilingual NLP pipelines with language detection and translation APIs, enabling seamless support across 15+ languages with minimal latency.
