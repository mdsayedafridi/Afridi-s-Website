Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Real-Time Synchronization

Implemented WebSocket connections with fallback polling for reliable real-time updates across all team members, ensuring consistency even with poor network conditions.

Challenge: Data Consistency

Utilized Firebase's real-time database with optimistic updates and conflict resolution to maintain data consistency across multiple concurrent users.

Challenge: Performance at Scale

Implemented lazy loading, pagination, and indexing strategies to maintain fast performance even with large datasets, enabling seamless scrolling through thousands of tasks.
